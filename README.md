# Herman Seip

**IT governance for public-sector leaders with stuck projects.**

AI has not changed the technology. It has changed the premises: building is no
longer the scarce part. Deciding, verifying and answering for what gets built
is. I help IT leaders get stuck projects moving again, with a strategy they can
understand and defend, and with AI put to work under control instead of feared.

## What that has looked like

- **A project stuck for two years, closed in 30 days.** Norwegian Courts
  Administration: narrow the scope, make ownership clear, reuse what already
  works.
- **From 1-2 production releases a year to weekly** for the courts' case
  system, on one shared delivery pipeline, with a DORA self-assessment as
  evidence.
- **Three systems computing fees three ways, replaced by one shared module**,
  now in production.
- **One shared event log instead of point solutions**, the same pattern in two
  large public bodies: Bane NOR and the Courts Administration.

## How I work

- Clean up first. Tidy goes fast.
- Make the good decision easy to take.
- The standard you walk past is the standard you accept.

## Governing AI, in practice

INIO is my lab: a small fleet of AI agents that build and run real services
under written rules. Decisions are recorded, every change is reviewed by
someone other than its author, a fixed list of actions needs a human go, and
when something goes wrong it is logged and turned into a rule. Agents make
mistakes. Governance makes them survivable and traceable.
